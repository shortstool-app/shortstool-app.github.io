ShortsTool — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11/14/2026

ShortsTool is a personal desktop application used by GEORGES Damien to publish his own videos to his own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube).

Data accessed. When the account owner connects his TikTok account, ShortsTool accesses: basic profile information (display name, avatar), account statistics (follower and like counts), the list of his own videos with their statistics, and the permission to publish videos he has approved.

Purpose. This data is used only to publish the owner's videos at his request and to show him the statistics of his own account.

Storage. Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's and YouTube's official APIs. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion. The owner can disconnect his account at any time in the application, which deletes the stored tokens, and revoke access in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
